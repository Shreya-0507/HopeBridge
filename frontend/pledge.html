Nasha Mukt Yuva For Viksit Bharat

A personal commitment towards healthy living, responsible choices and a drug-free India.

MY COMMITMENT

I, do hereby pledge that

🌿

I will not consume any harmful or intoxicating substance in any form or for any purpose, and I will also encourage others to stay away from intoxicants.

I resolve to make an active contribution towards building a Nasha Mukt Bharat (Drug-Free India), and to make my family, friends, relatives, neighbours and every person I come in contact with aware of the ill effects of substance abuse.

I will remain dedicated to building a healthy, aware and empowered youth power, and will contribute fully towards realising the resolve of "Nasha Mukt Yuva for Viksit Bharat".

I will always uphold healthy values in life, carry the message of drug-free living to every individual in society, and work continuously towards building an aware, empowered and drug-free India.

With responsibility, awareness and hope Jai Hind!
